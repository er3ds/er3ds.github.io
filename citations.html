Recent methods for registration, parameterization and statistical analysis

ER3DS builds on recent work in elastic shape analysis. This page lists the methods from 2010 on that the software uses for registration, parameterization and statistical analysis, taken from the same reference list that the program shows in its tabs and prints with er3ds references. If you use ER3DS in your work, please cite the papers of the methods you used.

How far the entries have been checked. Of the 17 methods below, 17 were looked up online (authors, title and journal details) when the entry was written, and 16 have a direct link. All of them are marked as checked.

A citation for the software itself is not available yet; if you mention the program, give its name and the version number shown on the download page.

Registration methods

  • Elastic-varifold matching: Bauer et al. 2021, Charon and Trouve 2013
  • Karcher mean and SRNF inversion: Jermyn et al. 2012, Laga et al. 2017
  • Geodesics in SRNF space: Jermyn et al. 2012, Jermyn et al. 2017
  • SRNF inversion: Laga et al. 2017
  • Elastic SRNF registration of disk surfaces: Kurtek & Drira 2015, Jermyn et al. 2012, Jermyn et al. 2017
  • Elastic registration, SRNF: Jermyn et al. 2012, Jermyn et al. 2017, Laga et al. 2022
  • Elastic registration to a mean, SRNF: Jermyn et al. 2012, Jermyn et al. 2017, Laga et al. 2017
  • Parameterization: Laga 2022 (code)
  • Toroidal parameterization: Sutti and Yueh 2025, Yueh et al. 2020, Yueh 2023

Parameterization methods

  • Spherical parameterization: Laga 2022 (code), Kurtek et al. 2013, Jermyn et al. 2017
  • Toroidal parameterization: Sutti and Yueh 2025, Yueh et al. 2020, Yueh 2023
  • Elastic framework for hemispherical (disk) surfaces: Kurtek & Drira 2015, Jermyn et al. 2012, Jermyn et al. 2017

Statistical analysis methods

  • Tangent PCA at the Karcher mean: Jermyn et al. 2017
  • PCA in shape space, as in LESA: Zhang et al. 2023
  • Elastic distance: Jermyn et al. 2017
  • Hierarchical: Jermyn et al. 2017
  • Bayesian: Zhang, Pati and Srivastava 2015
  • Mode-based: Deng et al. 2022a, Deng et al. 2022b
  • Shape models with a regression phase and an index function: Bhadra and Srivastava 2024
  • Longitudinal elastic shape analysis: Zhang et al. 2023
