Software for statistical shape analysis

Elastic registration and shape statistics for 3D surfaces

ER3DS finds, for every surface in a set, the point-to-point correspondence with all the others. With that correspondence it computes the mean shape, the principal ways in which the shapes vary, clusters, regressions, time trends, and paths between shapes. One window for the whole workflow, and the same engine on the command line. Please Note: This Website is still under development. Any questions can be directed to nith.sivakanthan@gmail.com.

Four kinds of surfaces

You choose the kind of surface at the top of the window, and every tab adapts. Count the handles of your meshes: that decides the type.

Genus 0

Closed surfaces without a handle: most teeth, many bones. Each mesh is mapped to a sphere.

Genus 1

Closed surfaces with exactly one handle: a ring, a vertebra with its spinal canal. Mapped to a flat torus.

Disk

Open surfaces with one boundary and no handle: a face, a tooth cut flat. Mapped to a disk.

Any surface

Any Surface using varifold distances. Nothing is parameterized.

What you get

Meshes in correspondence

Registered meshes in which vertices correspond across a sample of shapes.

Statistics

Principal components analysis, clustering with several methods, regression/classification using shape, and time models.

Figures and tables

Figures and tables documenting every step, and the results as NumPy arrays convenitent for your own scripts.

Visualization

Built-in visualization of mesh outputs, geodesic paths between shapes, and image/gif/video creation for presentation.

Example output

Real output of the program for three vertebra meshes. Three shapes are enough to show what the results look like, not to draw conclusions: a real study needs many more.
